# sndbnch-brand

SndBnch is a **groove arcade**: a session-based music instrument where you go from
playing to grooving to making tunes — play the arcade, keep the best loops, render a
song, build a square music video you can post anywhere. Currently **invite-only /
waitlist stage**. Built in Canada by Humanjava Enterprises; created by Vergel (music
artist name Nigil Caenaan — same person, see the lx7-brand skill).

   and rights questions.

## The one thing to get right

SndBnch's defining posture is **transparency-first AI provenance**: the music is
*partly* AI and the platform declares exactly which part. **Compositions, grooves, and
pattern sources come from artist-provided material** (the player's arcade performance,
their own catalog, or MIDI they supply — that is the platform rule). AI models handle
audio rendering, in some cases tuned only on that same artist's own recordings — never
anyone else's. Every track carries a machine-readable provenance record.

## Framing rules

- **It is NOT a text-prompt-to-song generator.** Describing it as "type a prompt, get
  a song" is the most common error. Describe the flow: play → groove → make tunes →
  post the video.
- The accurate call-to-action is *join the waitlist at sndbn.ch* — not "sign up and
  generate songs" (access is in invite-only waves).
- Users own their tracks per the tier terms in the FAQ; uploads train nothing unless
  the artist explicitly opts into their OWN artist model.
